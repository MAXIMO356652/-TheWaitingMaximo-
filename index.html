ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Meet Maximo — THE WAITING-MAXIMO.

Writing as THE WAITING-MAXIMO, Maximo writes for people who are still learning, still growing and still trying to understand their place in a world that does not always provide easy answers.

THE WAITING-MAXIMO represents patience while becoming, endurance while building, and continuing to learn while life unfolds.
